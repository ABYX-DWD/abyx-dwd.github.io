Keep your data up to date

To help ABYX prepare rallies and upcoming events more efficiently, we use a shared spreadsheet containing your vehicle power, rally size and APC troop type.

This information is not used to track anyone’s progression. It feeds our alliance tools, including Franky Rally Tool, and helps us prepare for future battles, events and challenges.

Whenever your stats change significantly, please update your information. Keeping your data accurate only takes a moment and helps the whole alliance.
